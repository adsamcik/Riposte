You're mid-conversation. You need that one perfect meme. Riposte gets you from thought to share in under 10 seconds.

Riposte is a meme organizer built for speed. Import your collection, let AI tag everything with emojis, then find exactly what you need with a tap or a few words. No cloud. No accounts. Everything runs on your device.

🔍 FIND MEMES YOUR WAY

• Emoji search — tap 🐶 to see dogs, 🔥 for fire content, 🍕 for pizza memes. One tap, instant results.
• Text search — type "confused cat" or "awkward handshake" and find matching memes by description.
• AI semantic search — describe what you're looking for in plain language. The on-device AI understands meaning, not just keywords.
• Combined search — mix emoji filters with text for precision. Find exactly what you need.

📱 BUILT FOR SHARING

• Long-press any meme to share directly to WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, or any app.
• Customize format (JPEG, PNG, WebP), quality, and size before sharing.
• Swipe through memes in detail view to browse your collection.

🏷️ SMART ORGANIZATION

• AI-powered emoji tagging — import images and get automatic emoji suggestions based on content.
• Import single images or entire collections as .meme.zip bundles.
• "More Like This" — discover similar memes using AI visual similarity.
• Fun statistics — track your collection with milestones, vibe checks, and sparkline charts.

🔒 PRIVATE & OFFLINE

• All AI processing happens on-device using MediaPipe and ML Kit. Zero data sent to servers.
• No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking.
• Your memes stay on your phone, period.

🎨 DESIGNED WITH CARE

• Material 3 with dynamic colors — adapts to your wallpaper on Android 12+.
• Smooth spring animations and a playful emoji-driven interface.
• Accessibility-first: full screen reader support, adjustable text sizes, high contrast.

Riposte is free and open source. Built by a meme enthusiast who was tired of scrolling through 2,000 unsorted images looking for that one reaction pic.
